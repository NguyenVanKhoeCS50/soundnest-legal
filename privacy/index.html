Privacy Policy

Effective September 5, 2026

Your privacy at a glance

SoundNest creates ambient audio on your device. Microphone access is requested only when you choose Record. Personal recordings stay on your device and are never uploaded. SoundNest does not sell personal data or show advertising.

Data we collect

SoundNest does not require or create an account. Firebase Analytics is on by default and collects coarse product interactions such as onboarding, playback, completed sessions, and purchase outcomes, plus basic app and device details, so we can improve the app. You can switch analytics off at any time in Privacy & Analytics. We never include ritual names or other text you enter in analytics. Apple and RevenueCat process purchase history; SoundNest never receives your payment-card details.

On-device data

Your theme, language, bedtime, timer, favorites, rituals, personal recordings, and sleep-session history are stored on your device. Recordings remain in local app storage until you delete them or remove the app. Procedural sounds are generated locally and continue to work offline.

How we use data

We use data only to provide SoundNest, restore purchases, prevent fraud, diagnose problems, and improve the product. We do not use your data for third-party advertising or cross-app tracking.

Service providers

Google Firebase provides analytics. RevenueCat and Apple provide purchase processing and entitlement restoration. These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies.

Retention and deletion

Favorites, rituals, personal recordings, preferences, and session history remain on your device until you remove them or delete the app. Turning analytics off resets the analytics data associated with this installation.

Children

SoundNest is a general wellness app and is not directed to children under 13.

Changes

We may update this policy when the app or legal requirements change. The current version will show a revised effective date.
